## Summary

This feature resolves two critical defects in the AI voice telephony intake workflow:
1. **Duplicate Booking on Time Shift**: Prevents premature calendar slot creation when a caller changes their time during a call (e.g. from 10:00 AM to 10:30 AM). Implements a two-layer booking defense: strict prompt deferral until explicit verbal confirmation, and backend call-session deduplication that updates the existing in-flight booking instead of creating duplicate appointments. Also enforces start/end time window disclosure with an explicit notice that duration is likely to extend.
2. **Returning Customer History & Contextual Recognition**: Enriches inbound call initialization with customer name, registered vehicle, upcoming appointment details, and past issue descriptions. Updates `get_customer_appointments` to return `issue_description` and `duration_minutes`. Enables Rachel to greet returning callers warmly by name and reference their vehicle and upcoming appointment once the caller states their issue. Supports consolidating multiple issues for the same vehicle into an extended appointment slot when calendar capacity permits.
